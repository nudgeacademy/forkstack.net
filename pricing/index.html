Clarifications

Frequently asked operational questions.

What happens when the internet goes down?
Billing, KOT, printing, table management and PIN login keep working on the main till. Orders queue locally and sync when the connection returns, and nothing is settled twice. The ERP, the reports and the AI assistant are online-only by design.
Is the price per outlet or per business?
Per outlet, per month. Core includes two outlets and Pro includes five; beyond that, additional outlets are 80 AED each.
Do I need to buy hardware from you?
No. ForkStack runs on Windows and macOS machines you already have, on Android and iOS, and in a browser. It prints to any network thermal printer on port 9100, and to any printer already installed on the machine.
How many staff accounts do I get?
Unlimited on every plan. Staff sign in to the till with a name and a 4-digit PIN, not an email and password.
What counts as an AI question?
One question you type into the owner app. The allowance resets daily. Pro has no limit.
Can I switch off modules I don't use?
Yes. Modules and individual screens can be switched off per organization, and a module that is off is off in the menu, the app and the API.
Can I move between plans?
Yes, at any time. Limits apply from the moment the plan changes; nothing is deleted when you move down.
Do you support multiple outlets on one login?
Yes. One organization account covers every outlet. Owners and managers get an outlet switcher; floor staff are pinned to theirs.
