Offline resilience

The till never stops billing.

When internet drops, table billing, KOT rounds, and printer queues keep running locally on the device. Orders queue in an outbox and synchronize automatically once connection returns.

How the sync protocol works

  • Nothing settles twiceEvery mutation carries a device-generated id, applied exactly once by the server.
  • Tenders never nettedCash, card, and delivery portals keep their individual line directly to the audit report.
  • One business dayVenues closing past midnight get one consolidated drawer and close, never split across two days.
  • Driverless thermal printingDirect raw TCP communication to port 9100 without OS drivers or print spooler crashes.
