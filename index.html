How it stays right

Every number came from the server.

The model picks the tools and writes the explanation. It does not do the arithmetic, and it cannot change what it reads.

  • 43 reads, 0 writesNo tool creates, edits or deletes a record.
  • Server-side arithmeticTotals and variances are computed before the model sees them.
  • 20 rows to the model, 200 to youTrimmed lists are flagged, so a partial count is never a total.
  • Draws nothing, not zeroA block with no data renders nothing at all.

Complete data

An answer is only as good as the day behind it.

The till does not drop bills when the connection does. Each device writes locally and drains an outbox when the link returns — nothing lost, nothing settled twice.

How the sync protocol works

  • Nothing settles twiceEvery mutation carries a device-generated id, recorded with its effects in one transaction.
  • Tenders are never nettedCash, card, UPI and each delivery platform keep their own line to the report.
  • One business dayA venue closing at 3am gets one day, one drawer and one close.

Cash-up

Short 200 and over 200 is not a clean day.

It is two problems that cancel in a total. Each tender is counted and reported on its own line.
