Ten rows, because Core is listed alongside the nine you can switch.

A module you switch off is off everywhere.

One registry drives the sidebar, the router and the server's request gate. Turning off Events removes it from the menu, makes its routes unreachable in the app, and rejects /api/events at the edge — rather than hiding a link and leaving the endpoint open. Individual screens inside a module can be switched off the same way.

The prefixes the till depends on are deliberately never gated this way. Menu, staff, attendance, expenses, waste, users, locations, POS and print are served to the POS app too, and blocking them would take an outlet's till down along with an ERP screen.

Who gets in.

The ERP is an owner's and accountant's tool. Managers, cashiers and kitchen staff belong in the POS and the Manager Portal, and the app says so rather than showing them an empty shell.

RoleReaches
AdminThe whole ERP, including payables, receivables, vendors, payroll, settlements, POS management, locations, the activity log and settings.
ManagerThe POS and the Manager Portal. In the ERP: the tax summary and day close only.
CashierThe till — floor, orders, payment, history. No ERP access.
KitchenThe kitchen display. No floor, no billing, no ERP access.
ViewerThe default role for a new account. Read access only, and no ERP until it is promoted.
