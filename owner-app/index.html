Three screens.

The assistant is the reason to open the app. The dashboard and payroll are what it is reading.

Assistant

Type a question the way you would ask a manager. The answer comes back as prose with the charts and tables that support it, and every figure in it was computed by the server before the model ever saw it.

The assistant is online-only by design. It reasons over live data; there is no offline copy of the business to answer from.

  • 43 read-only tools. Sales, money, stock, people, events and operations. Listed in full below.
  • Charts and tables, not just text. An answer renders the same blocks the dashboard uses.
  • Deep links. A figure in an answer points at the screen it came from.
  • No writes. There is no tool that creates, edits or deletes anything.

Dashboard

The business at a glance, filtered to today, a period, or all time, and to one outlet or all of them.

A card with no data does not render. There are no empty axes and no confident zeroes.

  • Revenue over time
  • Revenue by outlet
  • Sales by menu category
  • How customers paid
  • Online sales by platform
  • Busiest hours
  • Top sellers, by quantity and by revenue
  • Stock against reorder level
  • Running low
  • Open bills and pending sales
  • Latest bills
  • Stock value, staff and outlet counts

Payroll

The month's payroll on the phone, per staff member, with the detail behind each figure — and the previous and next month one tap away.

  • Days present, half days, absent — straight from the attendance register.
  • Base earned — monthly salary or daily wage, whichever the staff member is on.
  • Overtime and bonuses, added on their own lines.
  • Advances and deductions, with part settlements and full settlement.
  • Net payable, and what has already been paid.

Every number came from the server.

The model chooses which tools to call and how to explain the answer. It does not do the arithmetic. Totals, margins, variances and averages are computed server-side from your records and handed to the app as data.

A block pointed at data the server did not send draws nothing rather than a confident zero. Charts with a single point, and tables with no rows, do not render at all.
