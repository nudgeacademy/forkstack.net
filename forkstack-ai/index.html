Inside the app

The assistant, and what it reads.

The assistant is the reason to open the app. The dashboard, sales and stock tabs are the live figures it answers from, there to check at a glance.

What it can reach.

Forty-three tools, all of them reads. This is the whole list, grouped.

Sales
Summary · trend · breakdown by order type, hour, category and cashier · sales by staff · order detail · top menu items · period comparison
Money
Profit and loss · tax report · cash register · expenses summary · waste summary · vendor payments · vendors · inventory purchases
Stock
Inventory status · stock movements · inventory usage · recipes · food stock · production log · bulk orders · deleted inventory
People
Staff list · staff overview · HR detail · payroll report · staff payment history · activity log
Events
Events summary · event detail · event customers · event profitability · event logistics
Operations
Live operations status · dashboard snapshot · pending approvals · outlets · POS config · POS devices · menu items · menu availability · organisation profile

Every number came from the server.

The model chooses which tools to call and how to explain the answer. It does not do the arithmetic. Totals, margins, variances and averages are computed server-side from your records and handed to the app as data.

A block pointed at data the server did not send draws nothing rather than a confident zero. Charts with a single point, and tables with no rows, do not render at all.
