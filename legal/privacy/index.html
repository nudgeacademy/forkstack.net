Privacy

What ForkStack holds, where it lives, and how to get it back.

Last updated 2026-09-07

Who this covers

ForkStack is sold to businesses. The restaurant that licenses it is the controller of the data inside it; ForkStack processes that data on the restaurant's instructions. If you are a member of staff or a customer of a ForkStack outlet, your questions go to that business first.

What the system holds

  • Operational records — orders, bills, tenders, KOT tickets, tables, menu items, recipes, stock movements, expenses, purchases and vendor records.
  • Staff records — name, role, outlet, wage terms, attendance, payroll runs, advances and settlements. Staff sign in to the till with a 4-digit PIN; PINs are stored hashed, never in the clear.
  • Customer records — where an outlet chooses to capture them: name, phone number, order history, lifetime spend and last visit.
  • Account records — the email addresses and password hashes of the people who administer the system.
  • An audit trail — who changed what, and when.

Every record is scoped to an organization and an outlet. One organization's data is never visible to another.

What is stored on the device

A till keeps a local replica of the data it needs to keep billing during an outage — the menu, the tables, open orders, staff and their hashed PINs, and an outbox of actions not yet sent to the server. It lives in the browser's IndexedDB, or in the equivalent store inside the desktop and mobile apps. It is pruned as it ages and is cleared when the device is unpaired from the outlet.

The AI assistant

The assistant answers questions using read-only tools that query your own records. It has no tool that creates, edits or deletes anything. Figures are computed on the server and handed to the model as data; the model chooses which tools to call and how to explain the result. Your question, and the data needed to answer it, are sent to the model provider for the duration of that request. The assistant is online-only — it does not run against the offline replica.

This website

This site sets no cookies, runs no analytics and embeds no trackers. It loads two web fonts from Google Fonts, which means Google receives the request for those files. The contact form does not submit to a server: it opens your own mail client with the details you typed, so nothing reaches us until you press send in your mail app.

Sharing

Operational data is not sold, and is not shared with anyone except: the infrastructure providers that host your instance, the AI model provider when you use the assistant, and anyone your organization's administrator authorises. Nothing is shared for advertising.

Retention

Operational records are kept for as long as the licensing business needs them, which for tax and audit purposes is usually several years. When an account is closed, an export can be requested before deletion. Local replicas on devices are cleared on unpairing.

Your rights

Access, correction, export and deletion requests are honoured through the licensing business, which holds the administrator account and can act on them directly in the ERP. If you need to reach us instead, write to privacy@forkstack.net and tell us which outlet you are asking about.
