Terms of service

The terms on which ForkStack is licensed and used.

Last updated 2026-09-07

The licence

ForkStack is licensed, not sold. A subscription grants your organization the right to run the software at the number of outlets your plan includes, for as long as the subscription is paid. The plans, their inclusions and terms are set out in your order form or service agreement.

Outlets and staff

Subscriptions are issued per outlet. Staff accounts are unlimited on every plan. One organization account covers every outlet — owners and managers get an outlet switcher, floor staff are pinned to theirs.

Billing

Subscriptions are billed monthly and can be cancelled at any time; the cancellation takes effect at the end of the paid period. There is no setup fee. Moving between plans takes effect immediately, and plan limits apply from that moment. Nothing is deleted when you move to a smaller plan — data beyond the new limits becomes read-only rather than removed.

Your data

Your operational data is yours. You can export it while the subscription is active, and you can request an export before an account is closed. We process it to run the service and for no other purpose. See the privacy page.

Availability

The till is designed to keep working without us: billing, KOT, printing, table management and PIN login run on the device and reconcile when the connection returns. The server-side parts — the ERP, consolidated reporting, and the AI assistant — require a connection and are subject to maintenance and outages. Unless a separate written agreement says otherwise, no uptime figure is committed.

Acceptable use

Do not resell or sublicense the software, attempt to reach another organization's data, or use the service to break the law of the jurisdiction the outlet operates in. Accounts found doing so can be suspended.

Fiscal and tax compliance

ForkStack computes tax from the rates and inclusivity settings your organization configures, and produces the tax and VAT summaries described on the product pages. Configuring those correctly, and meeting the fiscal, invoicing and record-keeping rules of your jurisdiction, remains your responsibility.

Support and updates

Updates within the subscription are included. Support is provided over email to the administrator account. Response commitments beyond that exist only where a separate written agreement sets them out.

Liability

The software is provided without warranties beyond those that cannot be excluded by law. Our liability is limited to the fees paid in the twelve months before the claim. We are not liable for indirect or consequential loss, including lost profit or lost data where you had the means to export it.

Changes and termination

These terms may change; material changes will be dated at the top and notified to the administrator account. Either party may terminate a subscription at the end of a billing period. On termination, access ends and data is retained for a limited window to allow export before deletion.
