Everything you need to know about ForkStack POS, ERP suite, hardware compatibility, and deployment.

point_of_sale What is ForkStack POS?

ForkStack POS is our core cashier terminal application engineered for high-speed dining table management, round-based KOT tickets, split checkouts, and ESC/POS thermal printing with 100% offline resilience.

analytics How does the ERP auto-deduct inventory?

Each menu item is linked to a Bill-of-Materials (BOM) recipe. When an order is completed at POS, the exact raw ingredients (e.g., flour, sauce, cheese) are automatically deducted down to the gram.

wifi_off Does POS work during internet outages?

Yes! Cashiers can continue billing, taking cash/card orders, and printing receipts uninterrupted. Orders are queued locally in IndexedDB and sync automatically to the cloud once connectivity resumes.

print What hardware is compatible with ForkStack?

ForkStack runs natively on Android tablets, touch terminals like the Essae POS-956, and desktop web browsers. Supports standard ESC/POS 80mm & 58mm thermal printers via Network (TCP), USB, or Bluetooth.
